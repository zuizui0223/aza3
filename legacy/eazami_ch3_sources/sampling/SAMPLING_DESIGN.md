# Sampling design — doctoral mainline

Status: 2026-08-20

## Principle

Collect only biological data that the existing public/meta layer cannot reconstruct.

Sampling is organized around three doctoral gates, not a broad East Asian taxon atlas:

1. **Aim 1 — source of variation**;
2. **Aim 2 — ecological function and fitness**;
3. **Aim 3 — fast colour-switching mechanism**.

## Aim 1 — population ancestry first

Legacy core 190:

| Taxon | Minimum populations | Individuals / population | Minimum total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| *C. brevicaule* | 4 | 15 | 60 |
| *C. irumtiense* | 4 | 15 | 60 |
| *C. pendulum* | 4 | 10 | 40 |
| *C. sieboldii* | 2 | 15 | 30 |

**Core = 190 individuals.** Then legacy controls proposed *C. lineare* 2×8 and *C. dipsacolepis* 2×8; full minimum 222, fuller 298.

Every sampled individual receives one immutable `individual_id` linking population, taxon/voucher, visible/UV phenotype where feasible, orientation, direct phyllary/spine/stickiness measurements, DNA, plastid companion, flow-cytometry/cytotype material, and later functional/molecular samples.

The purpose is to discriminate standing ancestral variation, introgression/gene flow and lineage-specific origin. RAD/resequencing is a shallow population layer, not the universal species backbone.

## Aim 2 — nested functional subset

Aim 2 is not a separate field campaign; functional plants remain linked to ancestry.

Legacy order:
1. head orientation first — natural angle; non-destructive reorientation/sham; repeated microclimate/rain/pollen/visitor/enemy/final reproductive endpoints;
2. W/coloured function second — ancestry-resolved visible+UV, effective pollination, abiotic response, antagonism and achene output;
3. phyllary/spine conditional third — only after direct botanical validation and a non-wounding manipulation;
4. stickiness lower priority in this historical design.

Repeated bouts are repeated measurements, not biological replicates. Individual, capitulum, bout and plant-season levels must not be collapsed.

## Aim 3 — flowering-stage mechanistic subset

Legacy design required at least two independent W/C transitions, with same-individual DNA ancestry, floral RNA at developmental stages, pigment tissue, calibrated visible/UV phenotype and voucher. Priority systems included *C. brevicaule*/*C. irumtiense* plus a second Japanese replicate. A freezer-label-only mapping was explicitly disallowed.

## Stop rule

Add a taxon/population/assay only if it changes one of:
- source of reusable variation;
- trait → interaction/protection → fitness;
- retained pathway → floral regulation → pigment/colour.
