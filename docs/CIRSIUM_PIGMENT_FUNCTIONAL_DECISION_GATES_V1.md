# aza3 — A decisive functional test after unresolved DFR/ANR and ANS/FLS gene trees

**Prospective, frozen reasoning design (19 October 2026). No new plant material has been collected; no floral reactivation, adaptive fitness advantage or enzyme function has been observed.** All real Cirsium experimental plans stay in aza3. anto owns only genus-free dispersal and history-dependent latent-accessibility theory.

## Why the biological test must now change

The 2020 *Cirsium japonicum* transcript source is real and source-verified. A small set of Park 2020 DFR/ANS-like transcripts passed local homology thresholds, but both MAFFT+NJ and approximate-ML WAG FastTree placed all seven query/configuration evaluations BETWEEN_FAMILY_ANCHORS rather than definitively in DFR/ANR or ANS/FLS groups. This is **reproducible phylogenetic non-identification** under a deliberately sparse and partially aligned nine-reference protein panel, not loss of pigment biochemistry.

A mechanistic assay must distinguish **catalytic resource preservation C**, **flower-specific developmental regulation F**, and **ecological/fitness output**, rather than equating coding transcript matches with C=1. The 2026 *C. brevicaule* and *C. irumtiense* original young-leaf transcriptome samples are still not direct flower-stage pigment assays, so no present-day Ryukyu specimen is assigned C/F states.

## Five distinct causal explanations and necessary observations

| History/mechanism | Biological distinction | Crucial discriminating measurement | Claim not permitted without it |
|---|---|---|---|
| A: coloured ancestral expression retained south | Pigmentation persists without southern evolutionary regain | Rooted multi-outgroup phenotype history, population- and cytotype-aware local pigment gene genealogies | Southern re-expression |
| B: floral regulatory shutdown with retained toolkit | Functional catalytic ability persists outside flower, floral regulatory switch silenced | Same-plant florally stage-matched floret and phyllary+leaf enzyme/metabolite/RNA assays; independent rescue or segregation | White lineage is functionally re-expressible |
| C: genuine pathway breakdown followed by de novo reconstruction | New pigment-related causal variant, not retained ancestral allele | Dated ancestral local haplotype sampling, genome-wide population structure and multiple gene trees, direct causal function | De novo regain or closed evolutionary window |
| D: introgression or coloured propagule immigration | Different genetic source/propagule history rather than resident white-lineage regain | Neutral nuclear ancestry plus local ancestry/genealogy and dispersal/seed flow controls | Resident lineage reactivation |
| E: alternative biochemical partition/plasticity | Light/defense flavonoids, polymeric proanthocyanidins, other pigments or environment differ | Controlled environment, LC-MS/HPLC with standards across tissues and stages; gene expression and flux controls | Anthocyanin structural gene loss or an evolutionary transition |

The five histories are not mutually exclusive across populations or loci. Current white-vs-coloured taxon-tip contrast does not determine ancestral direction: the 0.93 Ma date in Chang et al. 2026 is a LINEAGE split, not a pigmentation event date.

## Phased assays — minimize cost and avoid false colour claims

**G0: source-to-plant identity and functional target validation.** Link independent samples, vouchers and taxon concepts, planned Ryukyu 75+75 Phase-A *if actually collected and permitted*, plus independent white/blue-purple var. takaoense matched populations if geography and phenotype are not confounded. Sequence gene-family candidates directly from the focal plant's DNA, rather than projecting Park 2020 TR39766/other assembly IDs onto Ryukyu individuals. Confirm homology, paralogy/homeology, cytotype and orthology before naming a specific protein.

**G1: compartment-specific biochemical organization.** Take matched floret pigmentation-onset and anthesis stages, outer/involucral phyllaries and permissible nonfloral controls from each independently identified plant. Measure VIS/UV reflectance and floral pigment chemistry as separate observations. Use standards and positive controls for anthocyanins, flavonols, proanthocyanidin-related chemistry, and other pigments; record tissue mass, extraction/recovery and detection limits. Concentrations from one snapshot do NOT identify metabolic FLUX or a pigment-defense trade-off. Flux allocation requires isotopic tracing or other independently validated rates.

**G2: candidate catalytic discrimination.** Compare recombinant/otherwise purified candidate protein with DFR/ANR and ANS/FLS positive biochemical controls; include extraction blanks, empty-vector/denatured negative controls, cofactor-specific conditions and multiple substrate/product combinations. For DFR, verify dihydroflavonol→leucoanthocyanidin chemistry rather than only similarity to short-chain reductases. For ANR, test anthocyanidin→flavan-3-ol products; for ANS vs FLS, quantify substrate-to-product profiles using authenticated LC-MS standards. Cross-substrate activity can occur: output is a PRODUCT PROFILE, not forced binary enzyme labels. Replication and protein expression QC must precede inferred absence of activity.

**G3: retained toolkit vs flower activation.** If functional catalytic machinery survives in nonfloral tissues but not florets, test whether a flower-specific trans-regulatory or cis-regulatory change can restore floral anthocyanin under appropriate staged/common-growth and permission-controlled interventions. Demonstrate actual floral pigment product and inherited state, not just a DFR/ANS RNA rise. If coding candidates are nonfunctional or unexpressed across all assay tissues, report C_STATUS_UNRESOLVED or CODING_DEFECT_CANDIDATE pending genome validation. This is where anto's latent C/F module model can become a real biological state.

**G4: capitulum LEGO coupling and ecological payoff.** Cross the experimentally measured pigment structural and regulatory states with same-head orientation and protective phyllary/stickiness modules. Test independent genotype/whole ancestry alternatives before claiming genetic LEGO. A proposed pigment-vs-involucre defense allocation must be tested using authenticated metabolites and agent interactions, rather than merely white-vs-coloured phenotypes. Under predeclared environmental contrasts, viable mature achenes provide the fitness outcome; trait correlations alone do not prove adaptation.

## Evidence gates that stop premature publication claims

- PUBLIC_HOMOLOGY_ONLY — 2020 transcript best-hit/motif and source family uncertainty; no Ryukyu pathway state.
- FOCAL_ORTHOLOGY_AND_ANATOMICAL_RNA — same plant tissue regulation observed, but no function or inherited restoration established.
- FUNCTIONAL_CATALYSIS_VERIFIED — candidate protein substrate/product demonstrated, but regulatory circuit and ancestry unresolved.
- FLORAL_REEXPRESSION_DEMONSTRATED — repeated, independently validated expression of pigment from a previously silent SAME lineage/circuit; still not ancestral evolutionary reversal unless independent rooted trait history supports it.
- EVOLUTIONARY_REACQUISITION_SUPPORTED — justified after historical absence/lineage retention/genomic origin, mutation and gene-flow alternatives distinguished; evaluate uncertainty rather than force one history.
- ADAPTIVE_HEAD_ASSEMBLY_SUPPORTED — requires independently validated source/capability AND head morphology assembly/replication AND causal/ecologically interpretable viable-achene fitness evidence.

A white flower can arise with an intact nonfloral pigment-related enzyme but a broken flower-specific switch; conversely a flower can appear coloured through other chemistry with no ancestral ANS reactivation. Neither scenario should be assumed before the matching assays.

## Concrete currently admitted molecular leads (Park 2020, NOT Ryukyu)

- TR39766|c0_g1_i2: relatively long DFR-like protein homology; four of five inspected *Saussurea* reference positions match, but one substitution, ANR homology and gene-tree ambiguity remain. **Best first source-backed enzyme-family discrimination candidate, not a proven locus**.
- TR35526|c0_g1_i1: retained DFR-like nearest-family rank across positive-reference omission, but low identity and weak N-terminal alignment coverage; choose as uncertainty/control contrast, not automatically an intact DFR.
- TR38875 isoforms and TR58302: family assignments sensitive to ANR/FLS-reference composition. **Do not discard** — they are appropriate positive tests of whether substrate/product function differs from nearest-sequence labels.

No one has measured these Park 2020 source candidates in the focal six Ryukyu plants. Independent plant accessions, reference genomics and floral catalytic assays must precede any gene-to-Ryukyu ancestry projection.
