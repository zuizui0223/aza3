# Orientation × spines: guild-specific access and adaptive capitulum configurations

**Status:** prospective cross-repository biological hypothesis; no focal selection or trait interaction has been observed.
**Date:** 2026-10-08.
**Research question:** Do head orientation and true involucral spines evolve as mutually dependent biotic-interaction traits, and when do they complement versus replace each other?

## 1. A nontrivial alternative to "spines deter visitors"

**Directional access filtering hypothesis (DAF):** Changing a capitulum's orientation changes the 3-D approach corridors through which legitimate pollinators, illegitimate floral-resource users, adult ovipositing seed predators, and stem-crawling antagonists encounter the involucre. The same botanical spines may impede different guilds differently after that rotation. Selection can therefore act on **joint orientation × spine geometry**, not on orientation and spine length as separable main effects.

This is deliberately NOT the claim that all nodding heads have long spines, that all insects dislike spines, or that attraction and defence always trade off. DAF is falsifiable against additive, substitution, ontogenetic and abiotic explanations.

A more surprising prediction: **strong local correlational selection can exist even when the pooled cross-species orientation–spine correlation is near zero**, because different antagonist access routes can reverse the selected combinations across ecological regimes. This prediction is not itself proof of correlated evolution, which requires independent genetic/phylogenetic evidence.

### Mechanistic sign competitors (no fixed preferred sign)

- **Complementarity / spatially guarded pollen corridor:** a particular nodding or erect orientation plus an effective spine barrier reduces antagonist *successful* entry while legitimate visitors retain pollen/stigma contact. The joint viable-achene effect exceeds the additive expectation.
- **Substitution / protective redundancy:** changing head orientation already prevents antagonist access or damages environmental exposure, so an additional spine barrier produces less marginal benefit; the joint viable-achene interaction is negative or near zero. Conversely, a spine barrier could release selection on orientation.
- **Both-punished / functional interference:** barrier placement and orientation together also obstruct the legitimate pollination route; the joint fitness interaction can be negative even if enemy attack is reduced.
- **Guild-dependent crossover:** the same O × S treatment helps in a flying external-ovipositor regime but is neutral or harmful where enemies crawl along the stem, attack from the upper head, or enter at the bud and their larvae feed internally. Butterfly is not automatically an antagonist and bumblebee not automatically a successful pollinator; behavior decides role.
- **Phenological stage partition:** a head moves from nodding at anthesis to erect after anthesis, while spine architecture continues to protect developing seeds. Ontogenetic reorientation can generate apparent between-stage "trait coevolution" without any heritable covariance.

Additional comparative anchors are established, not novel:
- Agrawal et al. (2000), *Centaurea solstitialis*, DOI 10.2307/3672545: spine removal released illegitimate Lepidoptera access, did not raise legitimate bee/fly visitation probability, increased their visit duration, and decreased filled seeds 22%; **not orientation × spines and not Cirsium**.
- Ohashi & Yahara (1998, 2000), *Cirsium purpuratum*, DOI 10.2307/2446309 and 10.1139/b99-182: floral display benefits bumblebee visitation but increases predispersal seed predation; no controlled orientation × spine intervention.

## 3. Three nested, separately falsifiable predictions

**P1 — current ecology: orientation rotates the defence's *selectivity*, not necessarily its overall strength.**
At a fixed developmental stage, species/locality and head size, the effect of a prospectively qualified external spine/access geometry proxy on *successful antagonist access per standardized observation effort* should depend on head angle and antagonist access guild. Legitimate-pollinator *effective pollen contact* may be unchanged when antagonist access is reduced. Report raw approaches, landings, conditional entry, effective pollen contact, oviposition/larval evidence, and filled viable achenes **separately**. Do not infer either guild route from overall visitor counts.

**P2 — ecological sign crossover: complementarity and substitution follow access route, not taxonomic names.**
The preregistered O × S contrast on the same viable-achene scale may change sign between independent populations dominated by aerial external ovipositors versus internal/stem-crawling adversaries; compare independent pre-outcome guild exposure and floral phenology, not post-treatment damage as the regime index. Include pollen limitation/availability and baseline reward/density. A crossover in total fitness is more consequential than a crossover in mere visit frequency.

**P3 — evolutionary response: ecological regime predicts O–S selection and genetic covariance better than one pooled trait correlation.**
If different guild environments repeatedly generate opposing O × S correlational selection, independently segregating populations / phylogenetically independent contrasts may show regime-dependent association, including zero pooled mean. Test jointly measured gravity-referenced anthesis angles and verified spine length/direction/mechanical barrier, with population ancestry, cytotype, shared developmental stage, and between-guild variation. Only ancestry-resolved replicate historical changes or experimental evolution could identify an actual *change in inherited coupling*; tips without direct spines cannot.

## 4. Primary design: natural-history gate before manipulation

**N0 morphology/behavior census — observation only, no collection authorization implied.**
- Identity and ontogeny: individual ID, head ID, locality, continuous gravity-referenced angle (0° up, 90° horizontal, 180° down), A0 bud/A1 early anthesis/A2 full anthesis/A3 late/P fruit stage, repeated same-head trajectory, plant size.
- Defence anatomy: direct botanical spine length, spine rigidity/force or standardized resistance, direction (3-D relative to floret entry zone), phyllary posture, access-gap geometry, stickiness as separate channel. Mark imaged outlines as *proxies*, not real spines.
- Behaviour: guild identified by action: legitimate visitor (anther/stigma contact and, where feasible, pollen transfer), floral feeder/nectar robber, adult ovipositor, stem crawler, larva internal. For each, approach origin vector, landing site, probe trajectory, successful access, contact, and oviposition; observe daytime and seasonal windows. A bumblebee, fly or butterfly can play distinct roles based on evidence.
- Keep pollen rain/wetness/temperature, UV and seed maturation as rival abiotic pathways; baseline herbivore/plant density measured independently.
- PolliPi fixed effort plus manually annotated event/approach videos for validation. Candidate counts cannot define an encounter rate.

**N1 design gate.** Do not advance until at least one focal population/species exposes meaningful, non-ontogenetic natural variation in angle and actual barrier geometry, access-route enemy observations, reproducible trait measurement, and site/handling approval. *C. sieboldii* is useful for the phyllary pilot but **not presumed** to vary in spine traits or host both orientation states within one population. A population that fails is a scientifically useful negative finding.

**N2 mechanistic factorial (only with approvals and device qualification).**
Use a 2×2 **assigned** O(orientation/sham) × S(external access proxy/sham) in a natural, safe range, split by plant/block and phenological stage; avoid clipping, piercing or damaging real spines and avoid device-induced orientation. If orientation and access cannot be independently manipulated without tissue injury or device confounding, the O×S causal interaction is **NOT_IDENTIFIABLE**, not observationally "rescued." Use the existing JPN36 n=24 phyllary-pilot design only for *single-factor feasibility*; it is neither powered nor authorized to become a 2×2 fitness experiment by relabeling arms. Final n must be powered after independent variance/feasibility pilots.

Factorial treatments also require: sham/weight/visibility controls, before–after angle and gap verification, individual-plant randomization if within-plant carryover exists, intention-to-treat, safety/attrition receipt, pollen and antagonist pressure context recorded before outcome, and independently reviewed taxon/phenology.

## 5. Registered contrasts and inference ceilings

For an endpoint `Y` on a declared, common meaningful scale, define the factorial interaction for orientation state O and qualified access-gap state S:

`D_OS(Y) = E[Y | do(O=1,S=1)] - E[Y | do(O=1,S=0)] - E[Y | do(O=0,S=1)] + E[Y | do(O=0,S=0)]`.

- Primary fitness response: filled viable achenes per head, with total potential achenes and reproductive head failure separately recorded. For count outcomes model expected seed count (and, separately, seed-fill conditional on ovule number), with population/plant clustering. A count-scale interaction is **scale-specific**, not invariant on log or logistic scales.
- Component responses: distinct models for legitimate pollen transfer (not simple visitation), adult oviposition/head intrusion, damaging larval infestation, and rain/UV/pollen viability. Interpret route allocation only from appropriate guild-specific observations/experiments.
- A response on the predator scale may be positive even when on the legitimate-visitor scale neutral, or vice versa. **Never subtract probabilities measured on biologically unlike denominators and label the result plant fitness.**
- Confirm signal against the additive O+S model, stage/ontogeny-only model, abiotic-only wetting/timing model, ancestry/locality/density controls and uninformative indiscriminate-access model. Split by population/year or other truly independent units rather than pseudoreplicate visits.
- A significant `D_OS(W)` establishes **current functional interaction**, not evolved genetic covariance, historical correlated transition, adaptive trait origin or coevolution with specific insects.
- Correlational selection needs genotype/individual variation and selection gradients across environments; a present fitness gradient alone is insufficient for demonstrating that an observed configuration originated by adaptation.

## 6. Explicit falsifiers and publication ceiling

1. No orientation-conditioned access routing despite real O and spine ranges; guilds enter from independent routes, or both receive equivalent interference.
2. All apparent O×S patterns vanish after A1/A2 control or repeated same-head stage alignment; apparent nodding/erect differences were ontogenetic.
3. Fitness response arises solely from rain/UV, not antagonist-specific exclusion: biotic defence mechanism fails, orientation may still be abiotic adaptation.
4. A current O×S pattern reproduces poorly among heldout independent populations / reverses only through post-treatment conditioning: no transportable biotic rule.
5. Clear O×S function but no repeatable genetic variation or comparative history: functional interactive phenotype, **no** evolutionary coevolution claim.
6. Strong selection but no adaptation-history evidence: present selection, not ancestral causal origin.

**Strong ecological result:** prospective factorial O×S × access-guild interaction predicts enemy entry and effective pollination and maps to viable-achene fitness, including a reproducible environment-dependent sign crossover. **Stronger evolutionary result:** the same guild regime predicts replicated O–S heritable covariance or transition dependence. These are separate hypotheses and neither is currently established.

**Immediate next public-data action:** an explicit small-sample null audit of existing authority-coded O/phyllary combinations to decide whether any tip-based correlated-evolution analysis is even identifiable. Do not threshold phyllary posture as spine length or run a phylogenetic CTMC on only eight jointly known categorical tips; code `NOT_IDENTIFIABLE_SPINE_CORRELATED_EVOLUTION` until direct botanical data are available.
