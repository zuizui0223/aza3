## Analysis order

1. **Sampling and identity.** Confirm >=2 authorized populations and >=16 admitted individuals per concept, with complete cross-layer identity.
2. **Within-taxon / cytotype QC.** Do not force mixed cytotypes through one diploid SNP matrix.
3. **Shared-locus sensitivity.** Quantify homologous-locus overlap and restriction-site dropout.
4. **Paralog/homeolog screen.** Remove loci with collapse signatures before cross-concept history.
5. **Ancestry and reticulation.** Estimate population structure/admixture and retain network alternatives.
6. **Sister stability.** Test whether JPN06–JPN15 remains a meaningful closest ancestry relation.
7. **History localization.** Remap stickiness and recompute minimum changes plus JPN06 terminal localization.
8. **Strict-locus final sensitivity.** No preferred placement may depend only on the permissive matrix.
9. **Decision.** Retain, revise, or fail closed using the frozen matrix.

## Primary falsifiers

The public history is revised if, after upstream QC gates pass:
- JPN06 and JPN15 are not retained as a stable sister/closest ancestry relationship across admitted sensitivities; or
- the JPN06 terminal concentration materially disperses or moves to an alternative edge; or
- the focal placement reverses under strict-locus, ploidy-aware or network sensitivity.

If cross-species RAD admission fails, report population ancestry/within-cytotype structure only and retain the Comp1061 target-capture scaffold for cross-species history.
