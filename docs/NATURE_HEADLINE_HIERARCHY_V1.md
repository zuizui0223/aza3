# aza3 headline hierarchy v1

## Too weak / already close to established modularity concepts

- different traits have different genes;
- phenotypic modules need not equal genetic modules;
- components of a complex organ can evolve semi-independently;
- different components have different evolutionary histories;
- standing variation can be reused.

These can all be true without changing the conceptual unit of evolution.

## Stronger claim tested by the current F/M/R design

> **Module boundaries are themselves evolvable.**

Operational meaning:

A phenotypic module can be a real, repeatable organization of visible traits and still fail to be a compulsory unit of genomic inheritance. Genomic effects can cross or break that pre-existing module partition, and the resulting reconfiguration can prospectively generate/predict natural trait combinations that the module-constrained inheritance model does not.

## Full Nature sentence

> **Complex organs diversify not only by changing modules, but by evolving the boundaries of the modules themselves.**

## What would make that sentence earned

1. Fig.1: the phenotypic modules are independently supported rather than invented for the genomic analysis;
2. Fig.2: component histories are asynchronously reassembled in the young radiation;
3. Fig.3: complexity-matched R predicts untouched phenotypes better than both global and phenotype-only local module-constrained inheritance;
4. Fig.4: pre-registered genomic mosaics correctly predict natural component combinations;
5. E4: under the same validation genotype distribution, R expands the reachable joint phenotype-combination space relative to module-constrained baselines.

## What does not earn it

- non-overlapping GWAS peaks;
- low genetic correlation;
- different local genealogies;
- one recombinant individual;
- R beating an incorrectly specified global module baseline;
- R winning only because it has more parameters.

## Mechanism below the headline

Standing variation, introgression, recurrent mutation and regulatory/homeolog reuse explain where the reconfigurable components came from. They are downstream mechanisms, not the headline claim.
