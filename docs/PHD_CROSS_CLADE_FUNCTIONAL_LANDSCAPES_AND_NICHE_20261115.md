# Beyond Cirsium: broad evolutionary theory, cross-clade originals, and the PhD's remaining novelty (15 November 2026)

**Status:** targeted source-checked comparative research; NOT a systematic literature census; NO new Cirsium measurements or causal experiments. This file links [24 source-controlled articles](../data/evidence/phd_cross_taxon_functional_landscape_literature_20261115.csv). Sources include original articles and openly labeled reviews/theory; conclusions from fish biomechanics/engineered flowers are **analogies**, not demonstrations in Cirsium.

## 0. What this search changes

**Three formerly attractive novelty claims are not new:**
1. **Functional exaptation & reuse**: *Dalechampia* 81 taxa already have a reconstructed resin-reward loss and apparent repeated return to floral defense (Armbruster et al. 2009); historical reuse itself is not a new idea.
2. **Trade-off mitigation / multispecies trait combinations**: Ohashi, Jürgens & Thomson (2021) explicitly develop a framework in which novel floral combinations mitigate visitor-related fitness valleys. Kay & Anderson (2025) explicitly frame adaptation as community-conditioned **floral adaptive landscapes**, rather than a single best pollinator. This general framing is not novel.
3. **Functional integration & modularity**: *Merianieae* 2019 already link modularity with shifts in pollinators and evolutionary rates; *Costus* 2026 has marked changes in form but largely **persistent efficiency-centered trait covariance/modularity**; *Nicotiana* genetic crosses and *Gorteria* mimicry show other axes of integration. Therefore phenotypic shape correlation does **not** prove underlying genetic parts are independent.

**A narrow, testable gap that remains in the retrieved set:** repeatably observing a **conditional form → multiple functions → viable offspring** mapping of gravity-oriented capitula and **real spine/opening** architecture, across nuclear-phylogeny-constrained natural contrasts, with same-individual developmental trajectory, relevant guild and controlled angle intervention. This is **a proposed integration and a taxon-specific gap, not a global first-priority claim**. Only comparing BM against OU, or finding a pollinator–predator trade-off, would be incremental given present literature.

## 1. Three compelling cross-clade insights

### A. Morphological convergence need not be functional convergence, and vice versa
- Wainwright et al. (2005) [DOI 10.1093/icb/45.2.256](https://doi.org/10.1093/icb/45.2.256) present many-to-one mechanical performance mapping in wrasse jaw systems: many skeletal geometries can produce comparable function. This is an **animal analogy**, not actual plant results.
- Dellinger et al. (2019) [DOI 10.1038/s42003-019-0697-7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-019-0697-7) show functional floral modularity and morphological shifts in Merianieae. Juárez & Kay (2026) [DOI 10.1111/plb.70277](https://doi.org/10.1111/plb.70277) find a bee–hummingbird pollination shift in sister *Costus* species with strong phenotype divergence but largely similar integration/modularity.
- **Transferable prediction**: given independent head orientation O and physical bract traits B, two natural morphs can have different O/B but indistinguishable defense access performance **within a predeclared equivalence bound**, or highly different defense performance despite similar O. Ordinary null-hypothesis non-significance is not functional equivalence. Measure a **vector** of outputs (effective pollen delivery, verified egg/larval damage, final filled achenes); equality of final seed count alone may conceal compensating costs.

### B. Ecology may select a changing trait-combination fitness surface, not one morphology
- Pérez-Barrales et al. (2013) [DOI 10.1111/j.1600-0706.2013.20780.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1600-0706.2013.20780.x) already demonstrated that showier *Dalechampia* bracts correlate with both higher pollen receipt **and** greater seed-predator egg laying; net selection tends to stabilize bract size. Song et al. (2012/13) [DOI 10.1007/s00442-012-2518-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00442-012-2518-2) demonstrated that *Rheum nobile* bracts improve warmth, UV shielding, pollen rain protection and pollination but can raise seed-predator losses. Thus the general “pollination–defense tradeoff” hypothesis is already well tested.
- *Pedicularis rex* Sun & Huang (2015) [DOI 10.1093/aobpla/plv019](https://doi.org/10.1093/aobpla/plv019) showed water-filled cupular bracts can impede seed predators under drainage manipulation, while observed legitimate pollinators did not discriminate; Sun, Armbruster & Huang (2016) [DOI 10.1093/aob/mcw097](https://doi.org/10.1093/aob/mcw097) found greater corolla exsertion linked to both pollen receipt and seed predation across 14 populations. **Water drainage is a multi-change hole intervention** and observational exsertion does not isolate the causal bract shape.
- Peng et al. (2019) [DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0213029](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0213029) used 3D printed flowers + hawkmoths to show a plant-side pollen transfer **proxy** and insect nectar-intake preferences can have different shape optima. Do not call these real plant seed-fitness landscapes.
- **Transferable prediction**: local microclimate E and *observed* mutualist/antagonist guild G can alter both (i) the optimal O/B combination and (ii) the tradeoff between pollen and seed damage. Analyze (F_{pollen}(O,B\mid E,G,t)), (F_{enemy}(O,B\mid E,G,t)) and viable-achene outcome separately. Use just **one** predeclared O×B term after pilot power gate, not an unidentifiable 5-way interaction.

### C. Time and community matter independently of ancestry
- *Silene colorata* (2016) [DOI 10.1093/aob/mcw136](https://doi.org/10.1093/aob/mcw136): nocturnal flower opening/scent/reward match night visitors; residual morning pollination may buffer nursery-pollinator moth *Hadena* when seed predation becomes costly. *Yucca*–moth (Pellmyr & Huth 1994) [DOI 10.1038/372257a0](https://doi.org/10.1038/372257a0): fruit retention decreases with high moth egg load and increases with pollination, showing same partner can impose benefit and cost.
- Comparative *Silene* (89 spp, 2022) [DOI 10.1093/icb/icac118](https://doi.org/10.1093/icb/icac118) finds climate–morphology direct and climate–pollinator–morphology indirect paths. A *Viscaria vulgaris* 27-population 2026 study [DOI 10.1093/evolut/qpag030](https://doi.org/10.1093/evolut/qpag030) shows quantitative turnover in local pollinator assemblage composition can predict trait divergence even in generalist plants. Direct vs mediated climate effects **are not themselves a new conceptual discovery**.
- *Aquilegia* Whittall & Hodges (2007) [DOI 10.1038/nature05857](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature05857) links spur evolution with pollinator morphology shifts; *Mimulus* Wenzell et al. (2025) [DOI 10.1038/s41467-025-57639-3](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-57639-3) shows early changes in bee attraction can outpace mechanical fit; partial phenotype steps are important even before full adaptive syndrome.
- **Transferable prediction**: stage-conditioned O(t) can reflect temporal function shifting; an ancestor's orientation/stage trajectory does not ensure same function under a new guild. But never infer adaptation to early enemy attack when head movement happens only after oviposition, or infer evolutionary reactivation from present function.

## 2. Revised dissertation thesis: from 'trait OU' to a conditional form–function mapping

**Proposed headline:**
> **When different reproductive architectures produce the same ecological function—and when a changing community favors different combinations.**

Japanese: 「同じ繁殖機能は異なる器官構造で実現できるのか――頭花構造の組合せ進化と相互作用環境による機能景観の変化」

This asks whether **conditional selective performance** is more conserved or predictable than raw morphology. The empirical system remains *Cirsium*, with external taxa providing **tested conceptual analogues**, not claim-ready independent replication. Separate two questions:
- **Historical**: Are O, B opening, real B spine, stickiness and their developmental trajectories reconstructed to change together, separately, or in certain orders? **Phylogeny + ASR + measurement error**; BM/OU only with suitable quantitative data/dated tree and real independent regimes. Existing EAzami O/B posture/stickiness cannot be promoted to a real-spine history.
- **Functional**: Does measured (W=f(O,B\mid E,G,t)) show interchangeable routes, genuinely synergistic effects or active tradeoffs? Compare within-population natural states first; use randomized non-injury orientation vs sham; only conditional permitted bract access intervention. Track **stigma pollen, oviposition, directly damaged filled seeds and final viable achenes** separately.

Three distinct outcomes to predeclare rather than seeking a positive result:
1. **Functional redundancy**: O/B morphology differs; several functions fall within predeclared equivalence bounds and viable offspring within uncertainty (needs enough independent plants; cannot use p>0.05).
2. **Ecological context dependency**: comparable O/B states exhibit different marginal responses in wet/dry sites or confirmed guilds, with holdout population prediction. Effects can be rain-mediated, visitor-mediated or enemy-mediated; do not force one pathway.
3. **Historical/developmental constraint**: correlation of O/B is driven by head mass, peduncle mechanics, phenology and ancestry rather than a functional optimum; controlled angle perturbation may show no material benefit.

**Critical limit:** OU in a 38-tip, morphologically sparse, reticulate young radiation does not estimate the current shape of a fitness surface from field experiments. Historical OU “trait optimum” and experimentally estimated **current conditional performance optimum** are fundamentally different estimands. Their agreement would be a **hypothesis**, not proof of historical selection.

## 3. Cross-system test opportunities without scope explosion
- **Primary organismal system (must-do):** within-species *Cirsium kiotoense* O and B variation plus matched-sympatric *C. kagamontanum* (verify actual site, shared flowering time, sex). Secondary *C. sieboldii* ontogeny and its putative upright flowering forms.
- **Comparative functional check (optional literature/public-data secondary):** *Pedicularis rex* protected portal / bract-water vs flower exsertion and final surviving seed. **The 2015 drainage and 2016 exsertion experiments use different individual cohorts**; do not multiply marginal results to fabricate within-plant outcomes. Use published effects only as independent predictions unless original matched individual-level data actually recovered.
- **Macroevolutionary independent contrast (optional):** *Dalechampia* defense/refunctionalization and *Rheum* abiotic protection offer testable analogues of functional pathways; neither shares a direct homologous capitulum B spine. No extra field programme is implied.

## 4. Five concrete execution gates
1. **G1 source & scope:** join focal voucher/taxon keys, 350 nuclear gene backbone and same-individual O/B morphology; keep fixed original EAzami history. Focal true spine data and new RAD delivery are still unverified.
2. **G2 measurement feasibility:** mark bud/anthesis/fruit heads, true gravity angles and genuine phyllary spine / gaps / rigidity; audit camera projection; sex, mass and plant display. If O×B has no joint support within strata, do not promise a compensation test.
3. **G3 model identification:** baseline BM for morphology; limited OU/phylogenetic niche tests only after trait coverage + dated ensemble + parametric distinguishability. External taxa are not merged into a nonhomologous trait tree.
4. **G4 natural-contrast mechanism:** real visitor/enemy observation effort and access pathway, pollen deposition, predation and filled achene endpoint; predeclared covariates and site overlap. Demonstrate actual guild differences, not inferred GloBI presence.
5. **G5 causal replication:** shammed random orientation manipulation and true floral outcome; include held-out independent site/year if power allows. Only if genuine enemy access plus non-damaging bract manipulation confirmed add one second intervention.

## 5. Stop/claim rules
- Systematic priority novelty cannot be asserted from a targeted search. Prefer 'unresolved in the checked studies' over 'never studied'.
- Do not use 'adaptive optimum' as synonym for OU AICc model preference.
- Similar plant seed counts can mask opposing pollen vs predation costs. Equivalence requires a predeclared meaningful margin and sufficiently precise estimates **on appropriate independent maternal units**.
- An intertaxon difference in O/B under different environmental backgrounds is not causal local adaptation. Current fitness payoff is not evidence of evolutionary origin without inherited trait differences and history.
- Extending claims beyond Cirsium needs **a new source-matched replicate independent clade**; a set of analogical citations is not replication.
- No user DNA or head field datasets have been newly collected/analysed by this document. No collection/permitted manipulation authorized.
