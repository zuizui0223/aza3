# Cirsium sieboldii Gate CS0 taxonomic diagnostic v1

Purpose: prevent apparent anthesis-orientation polymorphism from being created by taxonomic mixture.

Operational taxonomy: for Gate CS, use the current National Museum of Nature and Science Japan Cirsium treatment. Record alternative synonymies separately. Do not merge taxa in the field when the proposed taxa differ in the exact focal phenotype, flowering orientation.

## Focal taxon: Cirsium sieboldii

Field-compatible supporting characters from the current Japanese treatment include:

- perennial thistle of wetlands and wet paddy margins;
- basal leaves persist at flowering;
- heads usually nod at anthesis and become upright after flowering;
- upright-flowering plants also occur, so orientation is not a diagnostic character;
- involucre campanulate to tubular-campanulate, about 11-29 mm wide when fresh;
- phyllaries about 8-10 rows, appressed;
- outer phyllaries narrow-ovate and at most about one fifth the length of inner phyllaries;
- glands vestigial and involucre non-sticky;
- operational Japanese distribution for this gate: Honshu and Shikoku.

Never use head orientation to confirm the taxon whose orientation is being tested.

## Kyushu confound: Cirsium austrokiusianum, Satsuma-maa-azami

The current Japanese Cirsium treatment recognizes this as a distinct taxon:

- distributed across Kyushu;
- flowering basal leaves persist;
- heads usually flower upright, but can sometimes point downward;
- overall appearance resembles C. sieboldii;
- stolon production is reported as a distinction.

Gate CS rule at Kashibaru and other Kyushu sites:

- if focal identity cannot be established confidently from permitted non-destructive observations, set taxonomic_confidence to unresolved;
- unresolved individuals do not contribute to primary colour-by-orientation occupancy;
- do not dig, pull, or disturb plants to expose stolons under the observation-only Gate CS;
- frequent upright flowering is not evidence that a plant is C. sieboldii.

## Hida confound: Cirsium hidapaludosum, Hida-kiseru-azami

The current Japanese treatment recognizes the historical upright-flowering Hida form as a distinct species:

- Hida region of Gifu;
- heads remain upright from bud through flowering and fruiting;
- involucre narrower, tubular to tubular-campanulate, about 7-18 mm wide;
- phyllaries about 9-10 rows and more ascending;
- the current account states that C. sieboldii has not been found in the Hida distribution area.

Historical Hida reports of upright "C. sieboldii" therefore are not admissible evidence for the focal within-species orientation substrate.

## Mandatory non-destructive photo set

Where visible from permitted access, record:

1. whole flowering individual;
2. flowering head profile with gravity reference;
3. involucre close-up;
4. basal-leaf or lower-plant view;
5. outer-phyllary diagnostic view;
6. any naturally visible runner or stolon-like structure without disturbing soil.

## Taxonomic confidence field

Use exactly:

- high: focal C. sieboldii diagnostic evidence is adequate for the primary gate;
- medium: likely focal taxon, but one or more important characters are obscured;
- unresolved: a relevant alternative taxon cannot be excluded.

Primary CS_GREEN and CS_GREEN_LOCAL_SET calculations use high-confidence focal individuals only.

Medium-confidence records may be retained for a separately labelled sensitivity summary. Unresolved records are descriptive only.

## Fail-closed rule

A locality cannot become CS_GREEN or CS_GREEN_LOCAL_SET because ambiguous upright-flowering plants fill otherwise empty phenotype cells.

If excluding medium or unresolved records destroys the primary substrate, the primary result remains not identifiable until focal identity is resolved prospectively.

## Taxonomic hierarchy rule

A global checklist synonym decision does not override this Gate CS operational separation when alternative taxonomic treatments differ in the exact focal phenotype.

This avoids circularity: collapsing an upright-flowering taxon into C. sieboldii before testing orientation would manufacture the within-species pattern the study is trying to test.
