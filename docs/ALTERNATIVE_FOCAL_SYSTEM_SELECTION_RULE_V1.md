# aza3 alternative focal-system selection rule v1

Status: prospective; used only if the current C. sieboldii Gate CS does not open the strong route.

## Principle

The Nature question belongs to the organ, not to C. sieboldii.

A focal taxon is useful only if it can discriminate whole-organ, phenotypic-module and reconfigurable inheritance.

Do not preserve C. sieboldii as the focal system merely because reference work has already been invested in it.

## Trigger

Open alternative-system selection only if the C. sieboldii Gate CS is:

- CS_RED_PHENOLOGY;
- CS_RED_NO_PRIMARY_SUBSTRATE;
- NOT_IDENTIFIABLE after the predeclared accessible routes are exhausted; or
- CS_AMBER with no feasible route to an untouched predictive sample.

CS_GREEN_LOCAL_SET may retain C. sieboldii for a claim-limited route and does not automatically trigger replacement.

## Prospectively ranked selection criteria

Score candidate systems before new genomic results are generated.

### A. Natural combinatorial substrate — highest priority

Required evidence:
- same-population or prospectively matched local-set variation;
- same-individual measurement of at least two head components;
- variation not created by flower stage, taxonomy or geography.

### B. Multi-trait module variation

For the strongest F-M-R test, prefer systems in which at least two frozen Azami multi-trait modules can be measured and vary.

A colour × orientation pair alone is useful for the visual mosaic test but cannot by itself establish R over M.

### C. Sampling and validation geometry

Prefer:
- at least one discovery population with enough individuals for TRAIN;
- an independent or spatially blocked untouched validation population/set;
- feasible total sample size after QC inflation.

### D. Genomic tractability

Prefer:
- stable mapping to the existing Cirsium reference framework or a cheap focal-reference route;
- manageable cytotype structure;
- no obvious taxon mixture.

This criterion is lower priority than biological leverage. A genomically easy system with no natural combinatorial substrate is not a Nature focal system.

### E. Functional bridge

Prefer a system where at least one admitted component can later be connected to function/fitness without changing the central Fig.3 test.

## Exclusion rules

Do not select a replacement because:
- preliminary GWAS signals look strong;
- one population happens to have cleaner DNA;
- a candidate has a published causal colour gene but no natural reassembly;
- the taxon is convenient but lacks untouched validation;
- the expected result favors R.

## Selection output

For every candidate, record:

- substrate evidence class;
- multi-trait module coverage;
- accessible TRAIN n;
- untouched VALIDATION n;
- taxonomic/cytotype risks;
- reference readiness;
- functional bridge;
- overall route class.

The replacement is chosen from these pre-genomic criteria.

If no candidate satisfies the strong route, the Nature claim is narrowed rather than manufacturing a focal system.
