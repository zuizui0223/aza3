**Status: 2026-10-18. Active Cirsium sampling/analysis design, NOT field authorization, an observed evolutionary reversal or adaptation proof.**

## Strict repository ownership

- **anto:** generic theoretical model of plant dispersal, historical retention, latent module availability, evolutionary accessibility and across-angiosperm falsification. Petunia and other non-Cirsium clades are independent comparative cases there.
- **aza3:** all active empirical Cirsium design and evidence. This includes the Ryukyu white/coloured complex, independent var. takaoense W/BP, C. sieboldii LEGO system, source-data intake, plant organ assays, population genomics, inheritance, experimental restoration and fitness.
- Prior Cirsium files in anto are versioned source-audit history only. The CANONICAL working biological files have been transferred without modification to aza3/data/anto_source_handoff/. Future Cirsium empirical work is entered in aza3, never independently advanced in anto.
- EAzami, Azami and chun keep independent frozen evidence boundaries. Genetic LEGO is a hypothesis, NOT proven by EAzami's repeated phenotypic change counts or by phenotype covariance alone.

## The two connected but falsifiable mechanisms

1. **anto — upstream supply:** plant seed/lineage/allele movement, founder sampling, drift, pleiotropically retained functions, gene flow and local losses determine the JOINT vector of potentially reusable genomic/developmental modules G at a place and time.
2. **aza3 — downstream assembly:** given G and environment E, regulatory, developmental and genetic interactions determine which capitulum configurations Z can be assembled, with ecological fitness W conditional on separate experiments.

Scientific schematic:

    dispersal + founders + ancestry + local selection + hybridization
                      ↓
        joint retained module inventory G  [anto]
                      ↓
        developmental compatibility/assembly map F(G,E)  [aza3]
                      ↓
       realised head module configuration Z  [aza3]
                      ↓
          measured ecology and viable achenes  [aza3]

Do not assume anto's supply mechanism proves LEGO assembly or that observed LEGO phenotypic configurations demonstrate ancestral genomic reuse. Availability and expression are separate estimands.

## Five mechanistic alternatives to test in Cirsium

- R1 ancestral expression retained in a colour lineage, with loss in other descendants;
- R2 genuinely reactivated retained genetic circuitry after a supported ancestral loss;
- R3 new coding/regulatory origin produces a similar colour again;
- R4 pigment-linked alleles exchanged through introgression into a local genetic background;
- R5 already-coloured plants disperse/establish, without any resident white lineage reactivation.
- Control: developmental/environmental plasticity or distinct pigments mimic visible W/BP.

Neither current taxon geography nor the 0.93 Ma lineage split resolves which colour history occurred. Sources taxonomically disagree on species vs variety delimitation: use dated taxon concepts. Young-leaf RNA does NOT assess corolla-specific regulation.

## Same-individual package (primary evidence grain)

Immutable individual_id, population_id, taxon concept, voucher-linked images, deidentified region, sampling stage, field permission ID and tissue barcode must link:

- **capitulum morphology:** calibrated floret visible + UV colour, floral chroma/lightness, head orientation in gravity coordinates, phyllary posture/geometry, involucre sticky/gland condition; leaf only as nonfloral biochemical control, not as the focal LEGO morphological system;
- **organ-specific molecule:** stage-matched corolla tube/limb and involucral bracts, leaf/root tissue where feasible, anthocyanin/other flavonoids via LC-MS/HPLC, transcription (MYB–bHLH–WD40 and CHS/CHI/F3H/DFR/ANS plus verified downstream glycosyltransferases), protein/proxy functional evidence. Real plastome/nuclear orthology, copies and homeologs must be distinguished;
- **genealogy:** same-plant nuclear SNP genotype, cytotype/relative DNA content, chloroplast context, genome-wide structure, local pigment allele genealogy, candidate causal cis/trans regulators, ILS and introgression alternatives;
- **function:** common environment and, when authorized, controlled crosses/restoration/perturbation plus stage-matched positive/negative controls and independently replicated phenotype and colour chemistry;
- **fitness:** predeclared ecological mechanism experiment (optics, thermal/radiation effects or pollinator behaviour as alternatives; for orientation wetting, phyllary antagonist access, stickiness arthropod filtering), final filled viable achenes, genotype/phenotype-by-environment interactions.

Do not label a plant W+ after detecting DFR/ANS leaf reads. Floral induction, coding retention and a heritable regulatory regain remain separate biological facts. No fieldwork, tissue collection or genetic manipulation is authorized by this file.

## Five prespecified identification gates

- **T0 — exact source and physical sample:** source-linked voucher/individual/taxon and permission; specimen-code suffix is not by itself an herbarium voucher. Preserve ENA MiniSeq vs paper NovaSeq discrepancy.
- **T1 — module accessibility:** directly measure coding/biochemical toolkit C versus flower-specific regulatory activation F in same plants across tissues; genotype and methylation where feasible. Nonfloral pathway use U may protect coding only, not necessarily flower activation.
- **T2 — history:** compare rooted flower-colour/variant histories under an independently validated time/phylogeny ensemble, standing variation, introgression and de novo null; distinguish geographic recolonization from resident lineage re-expression. Dated splits do not equal pigment change dates.
- **T3 — genetic LEGO:** quantify joint inheritance and local genealogy of orientation, phyllary, stickiness, colour and head architecture in the SAME genotyped individuals, after adjusting genome-wide kinship, site, ancestry, cytotype, sampling/observer effects and phylogenetic placement. Primary candidate C. sieboldii. Species-tip modular transitions do not imply independent genomic modules.
- **T4 — realized assembly and adaptation:** genotype-matched recombinant or naturally segregating module configurations must exist beyond the single-ancestry null and be reproduced in independent populations. Test morphology-function-fitness causally before saying adaptive assembly.

Stop labels include NO_FLOWER_ASSAY, MODULE_STATES_UNIDENTIFIABLE, HISTORY_NOT_POLARIZED, LEGO_ONLY_PHENOTYPE, INSUFFICIENT_POWER, FITNESS_NOT_DEMONSTRATED. Do not silently upgrade them.

## Decisive hypotheses and falsifiers

H-accessibility: historical dispersal and module loss alter measured inventory G. Falsifier: no reproducible conditional history signal after measurement/ancestry uncertainty, or no predictive improvement over visible phenotype and environment baselines.

H-organ retention: nonfloral biochemical usage protects structural circuitry after flower whitening. Falsifier: usage has no effect once shared ancestry/ploidy and environmental conditions are controlled; pathway genes could be preserved for unrelated reasons.

H-LEGO: different head modules carry at least partly separable inheritance/gene histories; new assemblages arise under sorting and recombination. Falsifier: one whole-genome ancestry gradient explains all module patterning with no independent same-population recombinant configurations.

H-combination accessibility: joint covariance matters for future three-way configurations beyond single-module prevalence. Falsifier: module marginal and ancestry-controlled independent predictors suffice across frozen held-out populations.

H-adaptive mosaic: environment-dependent fitness consequences for a concrete configuration must be demonstrated. Falsifier: no predeclared viable-achene contrast or mediator support. A genetic or phenotypic association alone is not adaptation.
