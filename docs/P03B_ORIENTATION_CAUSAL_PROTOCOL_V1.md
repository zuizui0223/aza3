# P03b orientation causal protocol v1

Status: **DESIGN FROZEN — NOT FIELD AUTHORIZED**  
Date: 2026-09-25

## Why this is now the priority

EAzami has reached the public-data ceiling:

- orientation repeatedly changes through the radiation;
- its present ecological correspondence is scale-partitioned;
- transition-level BIO15/BIO1 tracking is real as a comparative pattern;
- an independent external-taxon public confirmation is not evaluable under the preregistered GBIF metadata gate;
- Azami image data are already-analyzed cross-scale evidence, not new held-out confirmation.

The next information gain is therefore direct focal-`Cirsium` causation.

## Core causal question

> Does assigned capitulum angle change reproductive fitness, and is any effect consistent with altered reproductive wetting/exposure rather than a device artifact or an unmeasured pollination/thermal pathway?

## Core randomized experiment

Use plants with at least two comparable heads at matched floral stage.

Within each plant, randomize heads to:

1. **NATURAL_SHAM** — natural angle retained; structure-matched support applies comparable material/contact burden;
2. **REORIENTED** — the same qualified device moves the head reversibly toward the opposite focal orientation domain.

The biological target angle/domain is frozen only after P03 own-data history identifies an ancestry-interpretable focal system. It cannot be selected from expected fitness results.

### Primary analysis

Treatment assignment is the causal exposure.

Achieved gravity-referenced angle is a manipulation check and secondary dose-response, not a replacement for intention-to-treat.

Plant is the randomization block.

## Split the destructive and non-destructive measurements

### Fitness cohort

Do **not** destructively sample pollen from the heads used for the primary final-fitness endpoint.

Required:

- achieved angle;
- direct wetting/water-entry record;
- head temperature;
- effective visitor contact;
- antagonist exposure/damage;
- mature achenes;
- filled achenes;
- final viability/germination where seed collection is authorized.

Primary endpoint:

> **mature viable achene output per assigned capitulum.**

### Mechanism cohort

Use separate assigned heads for assays that would remove reproductive material.

Required:

- achieved angle;
- natural or standardized wetting exposure;
- wetting duration/water entry;
- pollen viability/germination;
- stigma pollen load where technically valid;
- head temperature.

This separation prevents the mechanism assay itself from manufacturing a seed-set difference.

## Wetting is the primary mechanism

BIOCLIM is not a mediator measurement.

The protocol requires direct wetting or water-entry measurement on the capitulum.

A standardized wetting challenge is permitted in the separate mechanism cohort only after water dose and timing are frozen before observing biological responses.

## Keep UV-B separate

UV-B remains biologically plausible but should not turn the first experiment into an uncontrolled three-factor design.

A UV-B subexperiment is opened only if a qualified optical manipulation changes UV-B without materially changing:

- rain/wetting;
- head temperature;
- display geometry;
- pollinator access.

Ambient UV correlations do not identify UV mediation.

## Competing pathways are measured, not assumed away

### Temperature

Record head/internal temperature in the same temporal window. The comparative BIO1 mismatch means a universal thermal rule is specifically not assumed.

### Pollination

Use effective floral contact or pollen transfer. Visit count alone is insufficient. Retain time-of-day information.

### Antagonists

Record contact/damage where present as a competing path. Do not reinterpret no damage as support for wetting.

## Device qualification before field execution

The manipulation device must demonstrate, in a treatment-blind technical pilot:

- repeatable angle separation;
- no reproductive-tissue compression;
- no visible injury;
- acceptable display-height/azimuth change;
- equivalent sham/treatment material burden;
- sufficient retention across the intended observation interval.

Numeric tolerances are frozen **after the technical device pilot but before any confirmatory biological outcome**.

Failure of the device stops the confirmatory protocol rather than triggering a redesign after outcome inspection.

## Sample-size gate

The final confirmatory N is not yet scientifically identifiable because focal taxon, eligible-head abundance and block variance are not frozen.

Before allocation, freeze:

- target focal system;
- minimum biologically meaningful effect;
- eligible paired-head census;
- variance/block-correlation source;
- precision/power target;
- final N.

Do not size the confirmatory study from unblinded treatment effects. If authorized material cannot meet the frozen gate, the work remains a labelled pilot.

## Authorization state

- field execution: **false**;
- tissue/seed collection: **false**;
- focal taxon/site: **unfrozen**.

This document is a scientific analysis contract, not permission to manipulate wild plants.

## Claim ceiling

A successful experiment may establish:

> a focal `Cirsium` assigned-angle effect on measured mechanisms and reproductive fitness under the tested context.

It cannot by itself establish the historical cause of all orientation transitions, genus-wide adaptation, convergence, or a universal rain/cold rule.
