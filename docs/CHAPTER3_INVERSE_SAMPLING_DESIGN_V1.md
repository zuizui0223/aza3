# Chapter 3 inverse sampling design v1

Status date: 2026-09-02

## Principle

Sampling is designed backward from the strongest claim that each Chapter 3 layer is allowed to make. The design therefore does not ask “how many plants can we collect?” first. It asks:

1. what ambiguity must be discriminated;
2. what biological replication makes that discrimination identifiable;
3. which measurements must be linked on the same individual;
4. what is cheap enough to bank now;
5. which expensive assay should remain closed until the previous evidence gate succeeds.

This is a nested design. One individual should serve as many compatible purposes as possible, but no small sample is promoted into a stronger inference than it can support.

## A. Core breadth — reverse from P01-P05

### A1. P03/P04 all-Japan floor

P03 requires a taxon-balanced own-data sensitivity across the 38 admitted Japan concepts. The frozen minimum is three independent wild individuals per concept:

- 38 concepts × 3 individuals = **114 primary biological individuals**.
- Where feasible, allocate the three as 2 + 1 across two populations rather than taking all three from one patch.
- These 114 individuals are for topology/network sensitivity and same-individual trait linkage, not population genomics.
- Record orientation, phyllary posture, stickiness, calibrated capitulum images, population, voucher/diagnostic images and cytotype status before genomic results are inspected.

The primary species backbone remains the target-capture framework. The 114-sample RAD product is a same-library sensitivity/network layer, not a definitive species tree.

### A2. P01 focal top-up — JPN36 / Cirsium sieboldii

To decide whether the phyllary change remains concentrated on a terminal lineage rather than reflecting one local population:

- minimum: **30 primary individuals = 3 populations × 10**;
- recommended: **40 = 4 populations × 10**.

The first three individuals are already counted in the 114-individual floor, so this adds 27 minimum or 37 recommended individuals beyond the floor.

Every focal individual receives the full same-individual phenotype package. Population replication, not a very large single-site sample, is the priority.

### A3. P02 focal top-up — JPN06 and JPN15

For each of C. dipsacolepis and C. lineare:

- minimum: **16 primary individuals = 2 populations × 8**;
- recommended: **24 = 3 populations × 8**.

This tests whether the published sister contrast survives population structure, lineage reassignment and network/admixture sensitivities. Each taxon therefore adds 13 minimum or 21 recommended individuals beyond the three-individual all-Japan floor.

### A4. Core total

Assuming the three focal concepts are included in the 38-concept floor:

- **minimum core primary n = 167**;
- **recommended core primary n = 193**.

P04 requires no new biological sample quota because it reuses the same linked individuals.

P05 also opens no new fresh-collection quota in v1. First measure calibrated phyllary dimensions from the core individuals. If coverage remains taxonomically unbalanced, supplement with rights-cleared voucher/herbarium images under a separately frozen direct-measurement plan rather than collecting extra plants simply to increase n.

Technical library duplicates should comprise approximately 5–10% of libraries, selected before outcome inspection. They are not biological replicates.

## B. M01 depth — reverse from E1, E2 and E3

The key change from the old 60–75 individuals per Ryukyu species plan is that the full population-genomic panel is **not the first assay**. The inexpensive material can be banked, but E3 sequencing remains closed until E1/E2 justify it.

### B1. Stage M1 — history + chemistry + expression discovery

Use **two populations per focal lineage**, so species is not completely confounded with one locality.

C. brevicaule:

1. Okinawa Honto;
2. one independently authorized Amami-group population, preferably Amami Oshima when feasible.

C. irumtiense:

1. Miyako, representing the northern-range side south of the Miyako Strait;
2. one core Yaeyama population, preferably Ishigaki or Iriomote depending authorization and RNA logistics.

For each of these four populations:

- 15 primary individuals for phenotype/photo + leaf DNA;
- if one visit is intended to create a final-quality bank, collect 3 extra technical reserves whose identities are frozen before lab QC;
- reserves may replace extraction/library failure only, never an inconvenient biological result.

Thus the M1 focal bank is **60 primary individuals**, or 72 physical individuals if three reserves are banked per population.

#### Pigment chemistry

Use 3 individuals per population nested inside the focal bank:

- 4 populations × 3 = **12 focal chemistry individuals**.

Colour photography alone does not identify anthocyanin chemistry.

#### Corolla RNA

For E2, use biologically replicated, developmentally matched corollas from all four discovery populations:

- collect 6 candidate RNA individuals per population;
- sequence 5 primary individuals per population after RNA QC;
- 4 populations × 5 = **20 primary RNA-seq libraries** at one frozen floral developmental stage.

One developmental stage is sufficient for the first species-by-population expression contrast. A second stage is a follow-up only if developmental timing becomes the specific mechanism under test.

The decisive feature is replication across **two populations per lineage**. One population per species is not admitted as a species-level expression mechanism because species and locality would be confounded.

### B2. E1 ancestral-state bracketing panel

The focal pair alone cannot identify whether pigment was lost or gained. Chang et al. 2026 resolves Arenicola as sister to Nipponocirsium, with Sinocirsium as the next bracketing lineage. Therefore E1 requires a nonfocal trait/chemistry panel rather than only more C. brevicaule and C. irumtiense individuals.

Minimum design:

- at least **four Nipponocirsium concepts**;
- at least **two Sinocirsium concepts**;
- ≥3 phenotype-confirmed individuals per concept;
- minimum **18 nonfocal individuals**.

If a taxon is colour-polymorphic, sample at least three individuals **per observed colour state**. Do not collapse polymorphism to one taxon-level state; doing so would recreate the species-tip compression problem that Chapter 3 is meant to solve.

These outgroups do not all have to be collected personally in the field. Fresh or cultivated collaborator material is acceptable for chemistry when identity, provenance, rights and developmental state are documented.

### B3. Gate from E1/E2 to E3

Do **not** open final population-genomic selection sequencing merely because the two focal species differ in colour.

E3 opens only if:

1. ancestral-state/pigment comparison is interpretable rather than H3 by default;
2. the candidate expression mechanism replicates across the two populations within each lineage;
3. orthology/reference/callable-region QC can cope with the large reported genome-size difference;
4. a neutral demographic/background analysis can be specified before candidate-region interpretation.

If these conditions fail, the study remains at E1 or E2. That is a valid outcome and saves the E3 sequencing budget.

## C. Final E3 population-genomic panel

If the promotion gate succeeds, expand to four island populations per lineage.

### C. brevicaule

1. Amami Oshima — terminal/deep-divergence endpoint;
2. one intermediate Amami-group population;
3. one southern Amami-group population such as Okinoerabu or Yoron when verified;
4. Okinawa Honto — main-island endpoint.

### C. irumtiense

1. Miyako — northern endpoint;
2. Ishigaki;
3. Iriomote;
4. Yonaguni — terminal/deep-divergence endpoint.

Final target:

- 4 populations/species × 15 primary individuals = **60 per lineage**;
- total **120 primary E3 individuals**;
- if collected with three predeclared technical reserves per population: 8 × 18 = **144 physical banked individuals**.

Twelve individuals per population may be used as a structure/QC pilot, but it remains explicitly a pilot and is not promoted to the final E3 panel.

The E3 sequencing platform is **not frozen yet**. Because the focal species have strongly different reported genome sizes, a cross-species RAD FST scan alone is not acceptable. After E2 candidate nomination, choose a lineage-aware resequencing/reference strategy or a validated capture/candidate-region design, and demonstrate orthology, mapping and callable-region comparability before cross-lineage interpretation.

## D. What the investigator personally needs to do

This is a distributed sampling design, not a requirement that one person travel to every Japanese population.

If personal field time is limited, prioritize:

1. the four M01 discovery populations, because RNA/pigment material needs the tightest protocol;
2. the hardest P01/P02 focal populations where phenotype must be scored correctly;
3. standardized training/materials for collaborators who obtain the remaining all-Japan wild samples and terminal island leaf samples.

Collaborator-collected samples are admissible only when the same identity, imaging, population, voucher and authorization fields are retained.

For M01 E0-E3, no bagging, artificial-flower experiment, single-visit pollination assay or long camera deployment is required.

## E. One-individual package

For core individuals:

- immutable individual_id;
- accepted/source taxon concept;
- population_id and deidentified locality key;
- voucher or voucher-linked diagnostic images;
- standardized capitulum images;
- orientation;
- phyllary posture and calibrated phyllary dimensions where observable;
- stickiness/gland-exudate state where observable;
- leaf DNA tissue/library ID;
- cytotype/genome-size status or explicit missing value;
- authorization/conservation record IDs.

For M01 focal individuals add:

- standardized corolla colour/spectrum;
- pigment sample ID for selected individuals;
- corolla RNA sample ID and frozen developmental stage for selected individuals.

## F. Analysis gates

**G0 — identity and authorization:** no collection until taxon, population, count and rights are frozen.

**G1 — core breadth:** analyze the 114-individual all-Japan floor plus outcome-blind P01/P02 top-ups.

**G2 — M01 E1:** distinguish loss/gain/unresolved history with bracketing taxa and pigment chemistry.

**G3 — M01 E2:** sequence the 20 primary corolla RNA libraries only if E1 remains interpretable and the samples pass developmental/QC rules.

**G4 — M01 E3:** expand/sequence the 120-individual population-genomic panel only if E2 yields a replicated candidate mechanism and the orthology/reference gate passes.

This structure is intentionally asymmetric: inexpensive field material can be banked early, but expensive inference is purchased only after the previous question survives.

## Stop rules

- three individuals per concept never become a population-genomic claim;
- inaccessible concepts/populations are not replaced after observing expected topology leverage;
- one population per species never becomes an E2 species-level expression result;
- a polymorphic taxon is never compressed to one colour state when individual data exist;
- technical reserve replacement is allowed only for predeclared technical failure;
- no E3 sequencing if E1/E2 fails the promotion gate;
- no pollinator or climate agent is inferred from population genomics alone.
