# aza3 post-Gate-CS panel reallocation rule v1

Status: prospective routing rule.

## Why this exists

The legacy Nature Phase-A panel allocates 40 individuals to C. sieboldii inside a broader 298-individual programme.

The frozen Fig.3 F-M-R test now requires:
- at least 40 usable TRAIN individuals;
- at least 20 untouched VALIDATION individuals for the prospective route;
- potentially more after genotype-only power / QC inflation.

Therefore the legacy SIE=40 allocation cannot simultaneously serve discovery and untouched validation.

## Reallocation trigger

Do not reallocate the 298-individual programme yet.

Reallocation opens only after:

1. Gate CS is CS_GREEN or CS_GREEN_LOCAL_SET;
2. the 8-individual own-WGS pilot is OWN_WGS_GREEN;
3. the genotype-only prospective power report is available.

Before all three, the 298 panel remains a planning envelope, not a sequencing commitment.

## If the trigger passes

First determine the required usable and collected C. sieboldii n from the frozen power/QC rule.

Then redesign the broader panel under a fixed priority:

1. preserve the focal C. sieboldii TRAIN/VALIDATION requirement;
2. preserve the minimum independent taxa needed to test whether the genomic architecture is lineage-specific versus repeated;
3. remove redundant broad taxonomic coverage before weakening untouched focal validation;
4. do not cannibalize the untouched validation set for discovery.

No other taxon is removed solely because its result looks uninteresting after genomic analysis.

## If Gate CS fails

Do not enlarge C. sieboldii.

Return to the ranked alternative-system search. The reference-readiness work remains reusable, but the Nature-central focal system changes.

## Claim boundary

This rule does not authorize a larger sequencing budget.

It only states how the scientific priority should be reassigned once the focal system and power requirement are known.
